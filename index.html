Bristol stool scale

A standard way to classify stool by form. Types 3–4 are ideal; 1–2 mean constipation, 6–7 mean diarrhea.

    Portion sizes and what ++ means

    ++ No FODMAPs at all — water, plain meat, fish, eggs, fats, salt, herbs and sugar. Portion doesn’t come into it.

    + Low at a normal serving. A small serve of a “high” food is often fine, and a large serve of a “low” one can still trigger.

    FODMAPs are fermentable carbs that commonly set off IBS symptoms. Tolerance is individual — use this to pick what to log and test, not as medical advice.
